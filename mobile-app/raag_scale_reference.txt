Format: Each raag entry contains:
  - Raag Name
  - Thaat (parent scale family)
  - Aaroh (ascending notes)
  - Avaroh (descending notes)
  - Swara Notation (Indian: S R G M P D N / komal=lowercase / tivra=symbol#)
  - Western Scale Equivalent
  - Notes on C (Western note names when Sa = C)
  - Scale Degrees (relative intervals)
  - Time of performance
  - Mood / Rasa
  - Vadi (most important note)
  - Samvadi (second most important note)

SWARA KEY:
  S  = Sa  (1st  — always natural)
  R  = Re shuddha (2nd)  | r = Re komal (flat 2nd)
  G  = Ga shuddha (3rd)  | g = Ga komal (flat 3rd)
  M  = Ma shuddha (4th)  | M# = Ma tivra (sharp 4th)
  P  = Pa  (5th  — always natural)
  D  = Dha shuddha (6th) | d = Dha komal (flat 6th)
  N  = Ni shuddha (7th)  | n = Ni komal (flat 7th)

WESTERN SCALE DEGREE KEY:
  1=Sa  b2=komal Re  2=Re  b3=komal Ga  3=Ga
  4=Ma  #4=tivra Ma  5=Pa  b6=komal Dha  6=Dha  b7=komal Ni  7=Ni
